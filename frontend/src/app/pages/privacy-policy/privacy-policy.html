Privacy policy

This policy explains how the 7irafy marketplace prototype uses account and professional-profile data.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

What this policy covers

7irafy is a course-project marketplace that helps clients and professionals find one another. It is not a payment service and does not process card or bank details.

Data used by the platform

Account information
First name, last name, email address, and the credentials needed to sign in.
Professional profile information
Biography, selected service categories, selected Moroccan cities, and profile status.
Marketplace activity
Information you provide while using marketplace features, such as job-related activity when those features are available.

Why we use it

  • to create and secure your account;
  • to display your professional profile in the authenticated directory;
  • to match directory filters with the services and cities you selected; and
  • to operate, troubleshoot, and improve this course-project prototype.

Who can see your information

Authenticated users browsing the professional directory can see a professional's first name, service categories, and service cities. Email addresses, sign-in credentials, and private profile-management data are not included in the directory response.

Storage and retention

Data is stored only for operating this prototype and is retained while the development environment and account remain active. This project does not yet offer an automated account- deletion workflow; requests are handled by the project team through the private course channel.

Your choices and rights

You can update your name and professional profile from your account. You may also request access, correction, deletion, or opposition to the use of your personal data through the project team's private course channel. Do not post personal-data requests in a public issue tracker.

Moroccan data-protection law 09-08 recognises rights of access, rectification, and opposition; the CNDP explains how to exercise those rights and submit a complaint. Visit the CNDP guidance.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the prototype gains features. The date at the top identifies the latest version.
